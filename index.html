Border Force

The first border game board game

Twelve trays each and ten seconds a bag. No detector — the only way to know is to open it. Finish searching and pass with time left and the other officer has to pass too, wherever they have got to.

Your own bench 12 trays each · 10s a bag · stolen goods Bags arrive on your belt and you work them. Before the shift you each hide five stolen items in the other's queue and tell them what they are — once, in words. Recovering them scores the square of how many you get.
Across the table 12 bags each · 10s a bag · swapped Your passengers' bags go straight over to Officer B and theirs come down to you, so you are checking each other's queue and neither of you has seen inside. No stolen goods: handing each other the bags is the interaction now.
